Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Overview

Ghost Protocol is designed to minimize data collection. We only ask for the information needed to operate the private bridge experience, such as wallet addresses and bridge status data.

Information We Process

When you use the bridge, the app may process deposit addresses, destination addresses, transaction signatures, selected amounts, timestamps, browser technical data, and connection status.

How We Use It

We use this information to create bridge requests, monitor deposits, process payouts, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and maintain service reliability.

What We Do Not Require

Ghost Protocol does not require account registration, email, username, or KYC information for the public bridge flow shown on this site.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Ghost Protocol team through the official support channel listed on the production site.
