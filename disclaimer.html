Risk Disclaimer

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Blockchain Risk

Solana transactions are public, final, and dependent on network availability. Congestion, RPC failures, wallet errors, or chain incidents can delay or prevent bridge completion.

Address Risk

You are responsible for verifying the destination address before starting a bridge. Sending to the wrong address, wrong network, or unsupported wallet can result in permanent loss.

Liquidity Risk

Bridge payouts depend on clean-wallet liquidity. If liquidity is unavailable, requests may be delayed, rejected, refunded, or paused until capacity is restored.

Privacy Limits

Veils Pay is built to reduce direct onchain linkage between deposit and payout wallets, but no privacy system can guarantee complete anonymity against every observer or data source.

No Financial Advice

Veils Pay does not provide investment, tax, legal, or financial advice. Use the service only after understanding the risks involved.
